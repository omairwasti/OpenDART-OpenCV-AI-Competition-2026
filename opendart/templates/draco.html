Technical state, image metadata and provenance


How the analysis is computed
The method needs the images that came before this one. Validated past-only state is restored immediately before the selected image, then that original frame is processed afresh. No current-frame target label or stored overlay supplies the result.
Early images may have no selected target. Resolved terrain is represented by a coarse visible region, not a physical centre. The final partial transmission shows only the received strip; missing extent is unknown. Exact states, identity status and hashes are available with each result.

Autonomous and operator-assisted tracking
The replay shows autonomous and operator-assisted tracking side by side. Operator assistance is one target designation on a reviewed development frame at 23:11:08.829 UTC, with no later manual correction. The technical mode name in the original replay is human-designated mode.
This browser-compatible presentation copy retains the original frame sequence. Spacecraft commands cannot change subsequent prerecorded images, so this replay tests perception and tracking, not real guidance.

Operator-assisted tracking has median IoU 0.871, n=6 on six eligible later regions. Autonomous tracking has the same median on the same six-frame subset. This does not show that operator assistance is more accurate.
Sample counts, exclusions and measurement limits
18 reviewed / 20 sampled: eight compact labels, eight resolved-region labels and two uncertain labels. The two boundary-clipped cases remained UNREVIEWED. They were excluded, not counted as failures, negatives, partial frames or zero-IoU examples.
Point-error medians are conditional on selection; the selected frames differ between methods. Two early autonomous targets were unavailable. Visible boxes describe the received terrain, not a physical centre or range. Uncertain labels support abstention observations only.
This small manually reviewed sample limits generalisation. No method was retuned after evaluation. The synthetic simulation remains the separate causal control evidence.
