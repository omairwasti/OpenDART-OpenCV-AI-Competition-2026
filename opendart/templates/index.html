Image feedback improves the tested approach, but the estimator's full-state covariance undercovers. Known target size supplies monocular scale. These are results from a synthetic planar model, not real DART guidance validation.

Evaluation scope, statistics and evidence

Two appearances, 12 seeds and four conditions give 96 guided cases. Each 12/12 condition cell has Wilson 95% interval [0.758, 1.000]. Conditions use matched seeds; the result does not establish general reliability. Extended-blackout cases accepted an image update again at 240 s. Empirical undercoverage limits any statistical EKF-consistency claim.
